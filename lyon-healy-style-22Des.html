Lyon & Healy Style 22

$25,000

An impressive Lyon & Healy Style 22 featuring beautiful natural Tiger Maple and exquisite gold decoration, with classical elements reminiscent of the Style 23. This harp was fully restored in 2016 and carefully maintained. The action was professionally polished andre-riveted. It has a superb, rich sound with beautiful overtones and a resonant, even tone, making it anexceptional instrument for both performance and collection. Serial number 2705, built June 20, 1924, this harp is a remarkable example of Lyon & Healy craftsmanshipand musical excellence.
