About Us

At Harpsale, we celebrate the timeless beauty and enchanting sounds of harps. Our journey began with a deep passion for music and an appreciation for the intricate artistry involved in crafting these exquisite instruments. Over the years, we have dedicated ourselves to curating a diverse collection of the finest harps from renowned makers such as Lyon & Healy, Erard, and Wurlitzer. Each harp in our collection is meticulously selected for its superior craftsmanship, tonal quality, and aesthetic appeal, ensuring that whether you are a professional musician, a beginner, or a collector, you will find an instrument that meets your needs and exceeds your expectations.

Our mission

To provide exceptional harps that inspire creativity, elevate musical expression, and bring timeless beauty into the lives of musicians, collectors, and enthusiasts. We are dedicated to offering expertly curated instruments, personalized guidance, and outstanding service to support every step of our customers' musical journey.

Our vision

To be the world's most trusted destination for harps, celebrated for preserving the artistry of fine craftsmanship while inspiring a global community of harpists to create, perform, and share the magic of music.
